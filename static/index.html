THE LANGUAGE, AT A GLANCE

TERMINATOR;

Always at the end

Read the validation rules +
  1. Begin with the datatype int, then an identifier and =.
  2. Identifiers begin with an ASCII letter and may contain ASCII letters and digits. The keyword int is reserved.
  3. The right side contains an integer or identifier, optionally followed by more operands separated by +, -, *, or /.
  4. Each arithmetic operator must have an operand on both sides. Unary signs, decimals, and parentheses are not allowed.
  5. End with one semicolon. Only one statement and one assignment operator are allowed.
  6. Spaces, tabs, and line breaks may separate tokens; they cannot split an identifier or number.
  7. Missing operands, underscores, and characters outside the defined alphabet are invalid.
